# Mord-Kaiser MC

Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge Origin project for a Mordekaiser-inspired kit.

Target environment: Forge 47.4.4 with Connector, Origins 1.10.2, and Apoli 2.9.2.

## Current kit

- **Mace of Spades** — primary key. Arms the next three melee hits; each hit gains bonus damage equal to 30% of Iron's Spell Power. Landing all three grants Speed I and Strength I for 15 seconds.
- **Soul Shield** — secondary key. Damage dealt fills the Soul Charge bar. The bar caps at twice maximum health worth of damage. At 33%, 66%, and 100% charge, Soul Shield grants 11%, 22%, or 33% of maximum health as absorption. Cooldown: 12 seconds.
- **Metal Shards** — tertiary/Extra Keybinds slot. Lasts 15 seconds, damages hostile nearby living entities within 3 blocks for 10% of physical attack damage every half second, and grants +3 armor plus +10% armor. Cooldown: 45 seconds.

The companion JAR tracks final damage, successful melee hits, timed effects, dynamic charge scaling, and the armor aura. The datapack supplies the Origin powers and HUD bars.
